Wednesday, September 2nd: Flooding, RSS, Accesibility, Word of the Day

Hello everyone!

As you probably know at this point, there have been many severe floods throughout central Nepal along the Bhotekoshi river. I've made a small blog post linking a few different news sources and resources for sending support. This is a huge tragedy that has resulted in over a thousand (and potentially many thousand) deaths so far. I encourage everyone to give a bit to help out.

Additionally, I've been making a few updates on the site the past few days. The first addition is an RSS feed for blog posts. For those unfamiliar with RSS, it's the orange button at the bottom of the page. You can take that link and copy it into your RSS reader to add it to your feed, or use it with a service like blogtrottr or feedrabbit to get an email when I make a new post.

I've also fixed the Nepali Word of the Day widget. I had initially intended to manually update it each day, and then quickly discovered I had pretty much no interest in actually doing that. Now it updates each day automatically.

The last changes I made were mostly just to the stylesheet. I used this as an excuse to learn about border images in CSS, which is why the main page looks like a cool scroll of paper now. I also made the nav a bit more readable, and then cleaned up the design a bit for mobile. I'd still like to go through all of the css again and do a clean refresh of it all, but I'll have to save that one for next time.

As always, thanks for reading, and I hope you have a great day!

- Basanta

Wednesday, May 13th: Some Site Updates

Hello!

It's been a bit since I've updated the front page, so I figured I'd give a little update about the site in general - what's new, what I intend to add in the future, that sort of thing.

The biggest new additions of late are comment boxes on the blog posts and reconfiguring the site for mobile.

The comment boxes were asked for a few times by a few different people, and since this site is hosted statically I was originally under the impression that it would be a pain to do without some hacky workaround. Ultimately I went with commentbox.io, a free embedded comment box service that was way less of a hassle to set up than I had initially expected. It does make you login to comment, but it supports SSO to sign in with Google or Facebook and all that so it's not terribly convoluted.

The mobile configuration may still be sort of a work in progress. I've made the screen a bit wider for viewing on small devices and set up the nav so it's not in the way of all the other content. I haven't tested with that many different layouts, so there's a good chance it may look better on some phones and worse on others. If it looks funky on your phone, please feel free to reach out and let me know!

Other than that, the big plans at the moment are to set up an RSS feed so that people can be automatically informed when I've made updates, and to fix the word of the day thing. I might make it word of the week at this point, as that may actually be not a huge pain to maintain. Also people would notice less when I don't update it.

Anyway, that's all for now. Thanks for reading, and I hope you have a great day.

- Basanta
